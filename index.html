What's inside

Everything one app needs to get you exam-ready

Every feature below is built around one real constraint: aspirants studying on patchy connections, shared devices, and tight budgets.

01

Study, then practice

Topic-wise lessons across Reasoning, Quant, English and General Awareness, each followed by practice in the real exam pattern — negative marking included.

02

Three ways to practice

Topic-wise quizzes for one weak spot, sectional tests by subject, or full-length mocks spanning your whole exam.

03

Learn from your mistakes

Every wrong answer lands in your Mistake Notebook automatically. Home tells you which topic to focus on next, based on real recent accuracy.

04

Works fully offline

Download your exam's content once and keep studying with no connection. Progress saves locally and syncs once you're back online.

05

English or Hindi

Switch languages any time from your profile — no reinstalling, no separate app.

06

Real current affairs

Dated current-affairs capsules and official exam notifications, each with a source you can check yourself — not a generic feed.

07

On-device AI assistant

Ask for a lesson explained differently, or a hint on a stuck question — a small model that runs on your phone, never sending data anywhere. Can make mistakes, especially with calculations.

08

Bookmark & revise

Bookmark any lesson or question to build your own revision list, ready whenever you need a quick refresher.

09

No account required

Use the app fully offline with no sign-up. Create a free account only if you want progress backed up across devices.
